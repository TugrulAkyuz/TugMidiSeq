# What's New in TugMidiSeq — July 2026 Update

## A brand-new look
TugMidiSeq has been redesigned around a warm "Eurorack / hardware-panel" visual language — a graphite module with recessed wells, raised lane surfaces, silkscreen-style labels, and a hand-picked 5-lane color palette. Everything from knobs to grids now speaks in one consistent voice, making the sequencer easier to read at a glance and more pleasant to work in for long sessions.

## Cleaner, more legible sequencing
The 5 lanes, step grids, and the note-visualiser (Satellite) have all been reworked for better clarity — improved contrast, tidier layout, and smoother on-screen playhead animation so you can follow exactly what each lane is doing in real time.

## Better performance under the hood
The audio engine and the note-display were optimized to run leaner, keeping the plugin light on CPU even with all 5 lanes active. Smoother visuals, less overhead.

## Rock-solid saving & recall
We fixed how the plugin saves and restores its state inside your DAW project, so your patches, MIDI-port choices, and lane settings come back exactly as you left them every time you reopen a session.

## Improved stability & host compatibility
Internal timing and real-time safety were hardened, and the plugin now passes stricter validation — meaning fewer glitches, more reliable playback, and better behavior across different DAWs.
